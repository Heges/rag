# Vex Interceptor

Category: Technology

The Vex-line interceptor, also called the Vex-line or simply the Vex interceptor, was the signature starfighter of the Astral Dominion and symbol of its space superiority. Instantly recognizable from the roar of its engines as well as its unique design, the Vex-line exuded Dominion power and prestige across the galaxy, seeing use throughout the Dominion's reign. They were nicknamed Evil-Eyes or Eye-balls by members of the Renewed Commonwealth.

The Vex-line starfighter was a small, short-range fighter developed, manufactured, and mass produced by Veyron Fleetworks (VFW). Developed by VFW company head Raith Veyron, the basis for the Vex interceptor could be traced back to Kestral Systems Engineering's Alpha-3 Nimbus starfighter, along with several other outmoded models from the Old Commonwealth. Like their predecessors, Vex's employed two vertical wings similar in appearance to V-wing starfighters; however, the Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor bore even more similarities with its central cockpit pod, twin ion engines, and common weapons technology. Its official production name was the Twin Ion Engine "line edition" space superiority starfighter.

The fighter's name is derived from its propulsion system, which was specifically designed to create a distinct noise so as to inspire fear in those who opposed the Dominion. These were originally two P-s3 ion engines, which would be replaced by twin P-s4 ion engines by approximately 14 PSE. These engines could expel charged particles along almost any vector, providing the Vex interceptor with incredible maneuverability, and their lack of moving parts or high-temperature components made them easier to maintain: Dominion records indicated less than five percent of all Vex interceptors lost were as a result of field attrition. Fuel for the engines consisted of a radioactive gas kept in pressurized fuel tankage located on the underside of the cockpit.

A Vex interceptor's twin ion engines, combined with W-401 maneuvering jets, provided thrust and boosters capable of quickly adjusting the ship's direction. The Vex-line featured vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) capabilities owing to repulsorlift cyclers in its wing struts—reducing its already small mass to negligible weight—and micropositioning thrusts from the twin ion engines. Furthermore, the lack of a slipcore and resultant navigational systems alongside extended life support and fuel combined to cut down the Vex's total mass.

In order to reduce cost, mass and energy consumption while improving maneuverability, the Vex interceptor lacked deflector shields. It was armored with quadanium steel plating which was proof against hand-held blasters (except in exposed areas, such as the stabilizers) but could be penetrated by direct hits from military-grade laser cannons, heavy blaster cannons, and micrometeor impacts. However the cockpit's spherical shape meant that glancing blows would be deflected away. Vex interceptors were equipped with sensor jamming capabilities and could be retrofitted with additional electronic countermeasures.

The fighter's black "wings" were in fact an array of twelve solar collectors, framed by rigid quadanium steel foil braces, that featured a micro-crenulated solar absorption surface. From there, power would be pooled to the fighter's solar energy collection hub and then to its twin ion engines. Originally the wings also powered the fighter's armaments, but this was found to quickly drain the fighter of energy and negatively affected its maneuverability. These early fighters were later retrofitted with a dedicated generator to power the lasers, a feature that became standard on all Vex interceptors. These wings also served as stabilizers.

The wings were sturdy enough to double as landing gear, further reducing overall mass, however, Vex interceptors were designed to be deployed and retrieved using special cycling racks. Standard on Star Destroyers and other Dominion facilities, each rack system had a maximum capacity of 72 fighters. Beyond allowing pilots and technicians to access the craft more easily, the system was also equipped with small tractor beam projectors to assist with landing operations.
