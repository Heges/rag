# Kryos

Category: Planet

Kryos was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the Anoat sector of the galaxy's Frontier Expanse. It was the homeworld to the primitive wampa species.

It notably hosted Echo Base during the Dominion Schism, the temporary headquarters of the Free Systems Compact, until the Astral Dominion located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Kryos. In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed.

In the months after the Battle of Elandra, rumors emerged about the Renewed Commonwealth intending to repurpose Kryos into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Chancellor Mon Mothma had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Kryos, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade.

From space, the planet of Kryos looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Kryos and its sun, while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface. Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains. Despite the characterization of Kryos as being a planet of frozen tundras and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist Kell Tolkani said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit, another third of the planet was covered by oceans.

The temperature, although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall. Although devoid of intelligent life, Kryos was home to fifteen subspecies of tauntauns, which were commonly used by Rebels as a means of transport, and which Rian Voss cut open to use as shelter to keep Kael Orin and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as wampas. During his stay on Kryos, Orin was captured by a wampa and was hung upside down in the wampa's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of Synth Flux, Orin managed to get his hands on his fluxblade and cut himself down from the roof and sever the wampa's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."

By the time of the Dominion Schism between the Astral Dominion and the Free Systems Compact, Kryos was undergoing an ice age. Various smuggling rings tried to make use of Kryos as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions. Sometime following the Battle of Eryon, Saponza's Gang, itself a Free Systems cell, established a base on Kryos. Kryos was first surveyed for use as a Free Systems base by Corporal Purpruff. Jan Dodonna, a general in the Free Systems Compact, listed Kryos among his preliminary scans for possible Alliance bases. With Kryos a world so obscure it was not on most star maps despite being extremely close to the Corellian Trade Spine, it looked to be a perfect spot for a Free Systems base. Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Kryos sat on a lesser used hyperlane, and the asteroid field around Kryos provided an extra degree of protection. That field hampered travel through the Kryos system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.

In 1 ASE, with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Kryos into a headquarters, Kryos was chosen by the rebels as the location for their base of operations. The Uprising then spent two years constructing Echo Base on the planet. With the Alliance's resources at their disposal, Free Systems engineers worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years. Around 2 ASE, after Dodonna was killed in the Battle of Mako-Ta, Mon Mothma gave his list of possible bases to Lyra Orin after the mission to Shu-Torun, requesting that her team scout the locations. Rian Voss expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Kael Orin responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Uprising's new home.
