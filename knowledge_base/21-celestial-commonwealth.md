# Celestial Commonwealth

Category: Organization

The Celestial Commonwealth, also known as the Grand Commonwealth or simply as the Commonwealth, was a democratic federal union spread across light-years of space. Due in part to its long history, different names were used for bygone incarnations or eras of the Commonwealth. The Old Commonwealth and the first Celestial Commonwealth were terms affixed to the original incarnation of the Commonwealth that existed until its fall to the Nullborn Dominion, which in turn fell in 1032 PSE, leading to the restoration of the Commonwealth in that year. Later, the modern Commonwealth that had been supplanted by the Astral Dominion in 19 PSE was also referred to as the Old Commonwealth. Additionally, the High Commonwealth was a term used to describe the modern Commonwealth during the era in which it had reached the zenith of its power, which lasted from around 500 PSE to around 100 PSE.

The Commonwealth was originally founded many millennia before the Battle of Eryon, approximately around 25,000 PSE to 20,000 PSE. The Astral Senate governed the Commonwealth under the Astral Constitution, and comprised thousands of senators representing the Commonwealth's member worlds. The First Chancellor was the title of the head of the Commonwealth, elected from ranks of the Senate to lead the government on Ceryon. An ecumenopolis or city-covered planet, referred to as Astral City, Ceryon was the capital of the Commonwealth and therefore the seat of the Senate.

The Celestial Commonwealth and its star systems shared sovereignty, and spanned a multiplicity of worlds from the Central Worlds to the Frontier Expanse, uniting a variety of sentient species, human and alien alike, under one banner. As its power grew, the Commonwealth fought many wars against various opponents such as Varkari, the Zygerrian Slave Dominion, the Nihil, and several Nullborn Empires. The Aether Wardens, a religious group of Synth Flux-sensitives dedicated to the radiant current of Synth Flux, was sworn to the service of the Senate, and as such the Warden Knights were hailed as the guardians of peace and justice within the Commonwealth. Centuries of conflict culminated in the fall of the Old Commonwealth, and the last war between the ancient Warden and Nullborn concluded with the restoration and rise of the modern Celestial Commonwealth, ushering in the Commonwealth Era which was an age of peace that lasted for a thousand years. The Commonwealth's history was officially reset, and the ages before this event were merged into a collective whole known as the Old Commonwealth-era.

The Commonwealth entered a period of decline after centuries of expansion and prosperity. Aided by the resurgent Nullborn Lords, the Secessionist Crisis divided the galaxy between the Commonwealth loyalists and the Secession Compact. The Commonwealth Legion was organized under the leadership of Warden Generals who led the Commonwealth Military against the Secessionist automaton Army during the Replicant Wars. However, the conflict had been orchestrated to allow First Chancellor Malrec—the alter ego of Malrec, the Dark Lord of the Nullborn—to control and deceive the Commonwealth in a dictatorial manner. With the end of the war, Malrec initiated the Great Warden Purge through Directive Black and transformed the Commonwealth into the Astral Dominion by declaring himself Astral Emperor. The Dominion Senate stood as the last remnant of the Old Commonwealth until it was disbanded by the Emperor at the height of the Dominion Era. In the aftermath of the Dominion Schism, the Free Systems Compact was fully reorganized into the Renewed Commonwealth, restoring the Celestial Commonwealth after a generation of Dominion rule.
