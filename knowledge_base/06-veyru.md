# Veyru

Category: Character

Veyru was a legendary Warden Grand Master who led the Aether Wardens through the time of the High Commonwealth, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Nullborn, and during the transformation of the Celestial Commonwealth into the Astral Dominion. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Veyru trained generations of Warden, ultimately serving as the Aether Wardens's Grand Master. He played an integral roles in defending the Commonwealth during the Replicant Wars, survived Directive Black, lived to pass on the Warden tradition to Kael Orin, and unlocked the path to immortality.

Born in 896 PSE, Veyru served the Aether Wardens for centuries, becoming Warden Grand Master and helping to teach many novices. During the High Commonwealth Era, in 382 PSE, Veyru helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Warden and the anti-Warden Path of the Open Hand cult. After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow, he decided to keep the living weapons against Synth Flux-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless, out of the Warden Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the High Warden Council to train Warden initiates aboard the Star Hopper, leading them and other Warden in fighting the Nihil pirate organization in 232 PSE. After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Veyru reappeared in 230 PSE on Corellia, where he helped his former Warden initiate Kantam Sy and other Warden battle the Nihil, accompanied by a former Warden who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Azlin Rell. As the Nihil had also weaponized the Nameless, Veyru hoped that Rell's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Warden find a way to fight them.

Veyru was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Commonwealth. A leading member of the High Warden Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Taren Vey and Ki-Adi-Mundi. During the Invasion of Navara, in 32 PSE, the maverick Arel Quin introduced Ardan Velgor to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Flux Heir. The Warden elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Orin too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Warden Code. After Jinn's death during the liberation of Navara, the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Veyru's continued opposition to Orin's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Warden discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Nullborn, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two, Veyru was convinced that at least one more Nullborn Lord remained active following Oren Valis's victory over the Nullborn apprentice Null-Lord Varek.

Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems—led by Count Serak, Veyru's one-time Warden initiate—threatening to secede from the Commonwealth by 22 PSE. Unable to defend the entire Commonwealth against this betrayal on their own, the Warden took command of the newly-formed Commonwealth Legion, with Veyru himself leading an army of replica soldiers against the Secessionist automaton Army of the Secession Compact in the first battle of the Replicant Wars, rescuing the Warden and Senator from execution. For three years Veyru led the Commonwealth military's war effort as a Warden General, determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Arel Quin, who helped Veyru learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Veyru's efforts against the Secessionists were undermined by Malrec, the Dark Lord of the Nullborn who conspired to destroy the Warden and restore the Nullborn to power. After the Great Warden Purge commenced in 19 PSE, killing thousands of Warden both on Ceryon and across the galaxy, Veyru confronted the self-declared Astral Emperor but failed to cut short Malrec' reign and consequently retreated into exile, leaving the Nullborn Master to consolidate his power with a new apprentice, the former Ardan Velgor-turned-Xarn Velgor, at his side.
