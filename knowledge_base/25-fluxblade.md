# Fluxblade

Category: Technology

The fluxblade, also referred to as a laser sword by those who were unfamiliar with it, was a weapon usually used by the Warden, Nullborn, and other Synth Flux-sensitives. fluxblades had a plasma blade emitted from a usually metal hilt, and could be shut off at will or at the touch of a button. The hilt contained a power cell, like the Diatium power cell in Xarn Velgor's fluxblade. It also contained a kyber crystal which had been attuned to Synth Flux by a Warden, and which amplified the energy from the power cell to create the plasma beam, as well as containing it within a blade-like field. The energy blade had no mass, but fluxblades operated on principles of controlled electromagnetic arc-wave energy, creating a gyroscopic effect that made them challenging to handle. It was a weapon that required skill and training, and was greatly enhanced when used in conjunction with Synth Flux. Though also used by the Nullborn, the fluxblade was synonymous with the Warden, with some in the galaxy believing only Warden could use fluxblades.

fluxblades were generally used for both offense and defense. A fluxblade could cut through virtually anything, from flesh to blast doors. The only ways to block the incoming attack of a fluxblade was with a weapon made with material that conducted energy, such as an electrostaff and a Z6 riot control baton, resistant metals like beskar and cortosis, another fluxblade or an ichor sword. When used defensively, a Flux-attuned could deflect blaster bolts with a fluxblade, and with skill, could even reflect the shots back toward the shooter or some other target. Experienced Warden could even employ their fluxblades to absorb Synth Flux lightning. Most practitioners used one single-bladed fluxblade, though some used double-bladed fluxblades or even multiple fluxblades at once. Despite lacking Synth Flux-sensitivity, the cyborg General Grievous was capable of wielding four fluxblades at once thanks to his advanced mechanical body and enhanced cybernetic brain.
