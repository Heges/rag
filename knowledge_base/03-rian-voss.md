# Rian Voss

Category: Character

Rian Voss, known only as Han until being given the surname Voss, was a human male smuggler and hero of the Free Systems Compact. He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the Silver Kestrel, a leader in the Free Systems Compact and an instrumental figure in the Dominion Schism during the reign of the Astral Dominion. Although he did not believe in Synth Flux controlling his destiny, Han came to believe in the cause of Astral freedom, joining Warden Kael Orin and Princess Lyra Orin in the fight for the Uprising. Following the war, Han and Lyra welcomed a son, Tal Voss, who became the dark knight Voren Kade after being corrupted by the shadow current of Synth Flux. Although their relationship floundered, Lyra hoped that Han, as Ben's father, could find a way to break the dark side's hold on their son. Rian Voss became legendary across the galaxy as both a smuggler and war hero of the Uprising.

As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Corellia, Han yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot. With a fellow scrumrat named Qi'ra, Han lived as a street thief with Corellia's notorious White Worms gang until an important deal with Lady Proxima went bad, causing Han to flee Corellia. He was given the full name "Rian Voss" by an Dominion recruitment officer while entering the Dominion Academy. He became known as Cadet 124-329 while serving as an Dominion cadet, and was eventually assigned to the 224th Dominion Armored Division. On Mimban, Han met the thief Tobias Beckett and wound up deserting the Dominion and joining his crew to steal unrefined coaxium from Kessel in order to pay a debt to Dryden Vos, a kingpin with the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Qi'ra, Han was forced to kill his mentor Beckett after a brutal double-cross. Han also became rivals and close friends with fellow smuggler Dalen Cor and the Brakkan Brakk, the latter who became his best friend. Calrissian owned the Silver Kestrel, a fast ship with which Han made the Kessel Run in just twelve parsecs and won from Lando in a game of sabacc.

With only Brakk remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Corellian smuggler, Rian Voss embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Morvak the Guld, a notorious gangster and crime lord of Talaris. After ditching a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, Voss owed a great deal of money to Morvak, who then placed a bounty on the smuggler's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have changed when Han and Brakk were hired to charter Kael Orin, Oren Valis, and the automatons RX-7 and Ceral-3 to Avelora, avoiding Dominion Dominion legionaries who were searching for Kael's automatons. However, as they find Avelora destroyed by the Dominion's new superweapon, the Void Core, Voss became caught up in the Uprising against the Dominion, rescuing Princess Lyra Orin, delivering the stolen Void Core plans to the hidden Free Systems base on Eryon IV, and saved Kael from the Nullborn Lord Xarn Velgor in the Battle of Eryon, allowing Orin to destroy the Void Core. Voss fought with the Uprising for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Dominion.

After the Battle of Kryos, Voss sought refuge with Orin—with whom he had fallen in love—Brakk, and Ceral-3 on Cloud City, which was operated by his old friend Dalen Cor. There, they were captured by Xarn Velgor as part of a trap to bait Orin to the city. Voss was frozen in carbonite and taken to Morvak's Palace on Talaris by the bounty hunter Korr Dane, where he remained encased in carbonite for months until he was rescued by his friends. After returning to the Alliance Fleet, Voss was promoted to the rank of general and was given command of the Pathfinders, a special forces squad, for the Battle of Elandra. Voss led the Pathfinders to Elandra, where he, Orin, Brakk, and the special forces team, with the help of the native Sylvans, destroyed the shield generator protecting the second Void Core that was being constructed in orbit. Their actions allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the superweapon.
