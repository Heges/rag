# Nullborn

Category: Organization

The Nullborn, also referred to as the Null Covenant, was an ancient religious order of Synth Flux-wielders devoted to the shadow current of Synth Flux. Driven by their raw emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Nullborn were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under Malrec, the Dark Lord of the Nullborn who achieved his order's goal of Astral conquest after a millennium of plotting. Within a generation, however, the deaths of Malrec and Xarn Velgor marked the end of the Nullborn Lords. Though the Nullborn ultimately went extinct with Malrec's demise, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient temples that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt.

Thousands of years before the Replicant Wars, the Aether Wardens was fractured by a schism that began when a rogue Warden and his followers turned away from the radiant current of Synth Flux, believing the dark side was the path to true power. Having relocated to the planet Korriban in the Frontier Expanse, their movement ultimately evolved into the Null Covenant, resulting in a war between the former brothers and sisters in Synth Flux as the Warden Knights rallied to the defense of the Old Commonwealth. Although the Nullborn were driven off of the Central World Ceryon by 5000 PSE, centuries of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the Old Commonwealth's collapse. Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the last war against the Warden, the Nullborn were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; Null-Lord Bane survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, Null-Lord Zannah, before being entombed on Korriban, which became known as Moraband. Henceforth, the Nullborn was governed by the Rule of Two, a new philosophy dictating that only two Nullborn Lords—a Nullborn Master and a Nullborn apprentice—could exist at any given time. As a result, the Nullborn were all but forgotten while the Warden focused their efforts on preserving the new age of peace under the modern Celestial Commonwealth.

The Nullborn remained in hiding for a millennium until the rise of Malrec and Null-Lord Varek in 32 PSE. Malrec, trained by Null-Lord Plagueis, hid in plain sight as Senator Malrec of Navara. Orchestrating the blockade and invasion of his homeworld by the Mercantile Union, Malrec maneuvered the Astral Senate into electing him to the office of First Chancellor. His entire chancellery would encompass the final years of the Commonwealth. Over the next decade Malrec sowed the seeds of discontent within the Astral populace with the help of his new apprentice, the former Warden Master Count Serak, who adopted the name of Null-Lord Tyranus and became the public face of the Independent Movement for Self-Determination. The Secessionist Crisis culminated in the formation of the Secession Compact and the creation of the Secessionist automaton Army which, in turn, compelled the Senate to support the establishment of the Commonwealth Legion. Jar Jar Binks, acting in place of Senator Valen, started a motion to grant the First Chancellor emergency powers.

The Replicant Wars began with the First Battle of Gerion in 22 PSE, after which entire armies of battle automatons and replica soldiers deployed across the galaxy, making war and devastation commonplace for the first time since the formation of the Commonwealth. In the final months of the Replicant Wars, Malrec sacrificed and betrayed Tyranus as part of his plot to turn Ardan Velgor, the Aether Wardens's prophesied Flux Heir, to the dark side. Playing on Orin's fear of losing his secret wife, Senator Sera Valen, Malrec revealed his deception and true identity, while claiming that the dark side was the path to immortality. Members of the High Warden Council, having learned from Orin's report that the chancellor was, in fact, a Nullborn Lord, attempted to arrest Malrec, but the chancellor resisted. Torn between his faith in the Warden and the fate he foresaw for his wife, Orin ultimately chose the Nullborn by betraying Taren Vey, allowing Malrec to kill the Aether Wardens's great champion.
