# Silver Kestrel

Category: Technology

The Silver Kestrel, originally designated YT 492727ZED and formerly known as the Stellar Envoy, and registered or known under various aliases and pseudonyms including Broken Bell, Corell's Pride, Close Shave, Fickle Flyer, Foul Matter, Graphite Skipper, Jin-Den Smoke, Longshot, Lost and Found, Star Princess, Sunfighter Franchise, Sweet Suprise, Meetyl's Misery, Wayward Son, and Victory Ring, was a Corellian YT-1300 light freighter built around 60 PSE. Earning a legendary status under their ownership, the ship was most famously used by the smugglers Rian Voss and Brakk before, during, and following the Dominion Schism.

During the Falcon's time in the possession of the smuggler Dalen Cor, it accumulated numerous modifications in the name of convenience and luxury, including painted exterior paneling and a large escape pod. However, during Rian Voss's famous record-breaking Kessel Run, in which he piloted the freighter for the first time, many of these additions were destroyed or lost, leaving the ship battered by the time Voss won it from Calrissian shortly afterwards in a game of sabacc. Its aged appearance still belied numerous advanced modifications to boost the ship's speed, weapons, and shields, including a slipcore engine among the fastest in the entire galaxy, enabling it to outrun Dominion Star Destroyers. It also included sensor-proof smuggling compartments, which were used during the rescue of Princess Lyra Orin in 1 PSE to evade Dominion Dominion legionaries. Afterwards, the Silver Kestrel saw further action when Voss chose to join the Free Systems Compact during the Battle of Eryon, where it was able to sneak up and fire on Xarn Velgor's Vex Advanced, allowing Kael Orin to fire the proton torpedoes from his T-65B Kestrel-wing starfighter that destroyed the Void Core.

After Voss was captured on Vespar, Calrissian took command of the Falcon once more to search for him. In 4 ASE, following Voss's rescue, Calrissian flew the ship at the Battle of Elandra, where it entered the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and aided in its destruction from within. Over the next three decades, the ship fell out of Voss's hands and was stolen by a succession of criminals and thieves, ending up in the ownership of the trader Unkar Plutt on Kharon, a remote world in the western half of the galaxy. The Falcon was eventually stolen by the Flux-attuned scavenger Nira, former New Dominion Dominion legionary Finn, and Resistance automaton BB-8 to escape the forces of the New Dominion in 34 ASE. Shortly thereafter, it was reunited with Voss and Brakk.

Following Voss's death soon after, the Silver Kestrel became a Resistance vessel piloted by its new owner, Nira, throughout much of the New Dominion-Resistance War. In 35 ASE, the ship was once again captained by Calrissian during the Battle of Nexaris, which saw a decisive victory for the Resistance and Aether Wardens, before being returned to Nira, who had been newly dubbed a Orin.
