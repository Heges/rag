# Pyralis

Category: Planet

Pyralis was a young, small planet located in the Pyralis system, within the Atravis sector of the galaxy's Frontier Expanse. Once a garden world nourished by the Bright Star artifact, its orbit was shifted when Lady Corvax unleashed the energies of the Bright Star in an attempt to return her husband to life. The resulting gravimetric duel between the gas giants Jestefad and Lefrani over Pyralis heated the planet's core, transforming the lush world into an imbalanced volcanic hellscape. Adapting to life on their devastated homeworld, the arthropodal Mustafarians evolved into two distinct subspecies that cooperated to ensure their continued survival. Legends of Corvax's search for immortality brought the ancient Nullborn to Pyralis, seeking the same secrets of eternal life. There, they built a temple over a locus in the shadow current of Synth Flux, above the buried ruins of Corvax Fortress.

Acquired by the Techno Union over three hundred years before the Replicant Wars, Pyralis's lava flows were mined for unique and valuable minerals. It was also home to the Black Sun crime syndicate, whose headquarters was located near the mining town that became known as Zitontown after the Falleen Ziton Moj ascended to leadership of the organization. As part of the Secession Compact during the Replicant Wars, the Techno Union on Pyralis produced battle automatons for the Secessionist automaton Army. Malrec had a secret facility on the planet where he intended to turn "harvested" Flux-attuned children into Synth Flux-talented spies. However, the Nullborn Lord had the base scuttled when Warden Knight Ardan Velgor and his Warden initiate, Rhea Sorn, arrived to rescue the novices.

Pyralis served as the final redoubt of the Secessionist Council until the newly-appointed Xarn Velgor, formerly Ardan Velgor, was sent by Malrec to slaughter the council, effectively ending the Replicant Wars. The Nullborn Lord was confronted by his wife, Sera Valen, and his former Warden Master, Oren Valis. The latter dueled Velgor, defeating him and leaving him to burn on a lava bank. However, Velgor was saved when Malrec arrived and carried him offworld. Pyralis then came under the ownership of the Astral Dominion after the Techno Union and its assets were nationalized. Malrec had new facilities built on the planet, where more Flux-attuned infants were taken and transformed into agents of the Inquisitorius.

During this era, Velgor returned to the site of his greatest defeat and, with the help of the ancient Nullborn Lord Null-Lord Momin, erected the massive monolith Fortress Velgor to serve as his personal sanctum, built on the site of the former Nullborn temple. Throughout the Age of the Dominion, Velgor used the planet to fulfill his master's biddings, with the fiery world becoming the final resting place for victims of the Great Warden Purge. In addition, Velgor also undertook personal business within his new realm, attempting to wield the dark powers of Pyralis to bring his deceased wife back from the dead.

Following the Dark Lord of the Nullborn, the Dominion took hold of the planet. Resource extraction efforts on Pyralis were taken over by the Mining Guild, although strict Dominion travel restrictions on the system made it difficult for the guild to turn a profit and mining operations eventually ceased entirely. Black Sun also abandoned Pyralis for a more low profile world when the Dominion established a garrison there. The Mustafarians resisted the Dominion occupation of their homeworld and attempted to drive them from Pyralis by launching multiple assaults on Fortress Velgor.

Although it hosted one of the earliest Free Systems victories of the Dominion Schism, Pyralis fell into obscurity following Velgor's demise at the Battle of Elandra, though Black Sun did eventually return to the planet. After the destruction of the Bright Star, the planet's ecosystem slowly began to heal from the devastation it had endured for centuries prior. Many devout pilgrims journeyed to the ruins of Fortress Velgor, hoping to tap into the late Nullborn's dark power. Decades after Velgor's death, his grandson, Voren Kade, journeyed to the world to continue the legacy of his family's grisly past. The Supreme Leader of the New Dominion slaughtered the Nullborn cult that defended Fortress Velgor and discovered his grandfather's Nullborn wayfinder, a tool that allowed him to locate the phantom emperor Malrec on Nexaris.
