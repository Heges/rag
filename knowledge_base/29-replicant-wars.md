# Replicant Wars

Category: Event

The Replicant Wars (22–19 PSE) were a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Celestial Commonwealth and the Secession Compact, which had seceded from the Commonwealth. Also referred to singularly as the replica War, they were named after the replica soldiers used by the Commonwealth Legion against the Secessionist automaton Army. Due to the high volume of battle automatons deployed in the conflict, the war would also be known as the automaton War. Furthermore, the existence of the Secession Compact granted the conflict other names, such as the Secessionist War, Secessionist Uprising, and Secessionist uprising. With both replicas and automatons being fielded in enormous numbers by each respective side, they were the largest military forces in Astral history, and fighting between them swept through the galaxy for three, nearly four, years.

Prior to the conflict, Malrec, under his alter ego Malrec, had spent years amassing political power, eventually using the Mercantile Union's invasion of Navara to become the Commonwealth's First Chancellor. Through his Nullborn apprentice Count Serak, he sparked the Secessionist Crisis, causing thousands of Commonwealth star systems to secede and form a new government as the Secession Compact, with Dooku as its Head of State. Beginning with the First Battle of Gerion, the conflict quickly spread throughout the galaxy with both sides scoring significant victories. At different points in the war it seemed that either side was likely to triumph over the other. At the war's outset, both sides vied for control of major slipstream lanes to gain strategic advantage. That particular struggle ended with a Commonwealth victory when Warden Knight Ardan Velgor and his Warden initiate, Rhea Sorn, secured an alliance between the Commonwealth and the Guld Clan.

Battles, skirmishes, raids, and other actions continued for the next two years, with notable battles occurring on Ryloth, Felucia, Malastare, and Pelagia. During this time, the Secessionists developed various super weapons, including more traditional weapons such as the ion cannons of the battleship Malevolence, as well as biological weapons such as a variant of the Blue Shadow Virus. Meanwhile, the Commonwealth launched several key offensives, including the Second Battle of Gerion, which obliterated Gerion' battle automaton foundries, and a failed Warden-led operation to capture Supreme Commander of the automaton Army General Grievous at Saleucami. Amidst the escalating conflict, many planets—including Mandalore and other members of the Council of Neutral Systems—struggled to maintain their neutrality, but found it difficult to escape the carnage.

Over a year into the war, Commonwealth Senator Sera Valen and Secessionist Senator Mina Bonteri collaborated on an initiative to end the fighting. However, their collaboration ended after Dooku and Grievous' bombing of the Commonwealth capital on Ceryon. Later, a peace conference on Mandalore also failed to resolve the conflict. With no end in sight, the Warden worked with local militaries, including Mon Cala's military and Navara's Gungan Grand Army, and covertly trained an insurgent Free Systems cell to retake Onderon from Secessionist occupation. Similarly, the Secession Compact relied upon factions such as the Umbaran Militia and the Zygerrian Slave Dominion. Furthermore, a proxy war materialized when Mother Talzin and her son, the now rogue Nullborn Lord Maul, strove to exact their revenge on both the Nullborn and the Warden.

During the war's third and final year, the Commonwealth drove the Secessionists into redoubts within the Frontier Expanse, initiating a campaign termed the Frontier Expanse Sieges. In an effort to reverse the course of the war, Grievous launched a massive strike on Ceryon through the Deep Core. Much of the Confederate fleet was lost in the assault, along with Head of State Count Serak, who was betrayed and sacrificed by Malrec as part of his plot to lure Orin to the Dark Side. The Secession Compact quickly lost its core leadership in quick succession, with Grievous subsequently falling in battle during the Battle of Utapau, and the Secessionist Council being massacred on Pyralis. This, coupled with the issuing of Directive Black and the deactivation of the automaton army, led to the total capitulation of the Secession Compact and, with it, an end to the war.
