# Ceryon

Category: Planet

Ceryon (pronounced /'kɔɹəsɑnt/), also known as Jewel of the Central Worlds, or Dominion Center during the rule of the Astral Dominion, was an ecumenopolis—a city-covered planet, in the Ceryon system of the Central Worlds. Prior to being covered by over five thousand metallic levels of what would become known as Dominion City, Ceryon was the ancient homeworld of the Taung and Zhell species. Though debated by historians, the theory that Ceryon was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers, Ceryon's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both humanoid and alien species. In addition, Ceryon's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city-planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of Astral culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Warden Temple, Monument Plaza, and the Senate Building.

Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Celestial Commonwealth, Ceryon was at the center of many historic events, such as the Aether Wardens's conflict with the Nullborn. Although the planet fell under Nullborn control for a time, Commonwealth forces ultimately liberated Ceryon with the aid of the Warden Knights. Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Nullborn became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Celestial Commonwealth. Retaining its status as Astral capital, it was on Ceryon where the Astral Senate governed the galaxy for centuries, establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Warden peacekeepers. However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Ceryon into a dangerous environment by the time of the Replicant Wars with the Secession Compact. On the eve of the New Order, fulfilling a grand scheme, First Chancellor Malrec, who was, in fact, the Nullborn Lord Malrec, declared the Warden enemies of the state, resulting in a galaxy-wide purge that all but destroyed the Aether Wardens. Without the Warden to oppose him, Malrec dissolved the Commonwealth and declared himself Astral Emperor to the applause of the now Dominion Senate.

Ceryon continued to serve as Astral capital under the Dominion, having become the Emperor's throneworld, although Malrec himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day-to-day governing to his advisors in the Dominion Ruling Council. The Dominion maintained a firm hold on Ceryon throughout the Emperor's reign, but with his defeat at the Battle of Elandra in 4 ASE by the Free Systems Compact, citizens of Ceryon celebrated by revolting. Unable to command the military's allegiance as the rebels restored the Commonwealth, Grand Vizier Mas Amedda's rule was limited to Ceryon while Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax, the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Dominion, assumed de facto control of the collapsing Dominion government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Amedda ultimately surrendered himself to the Renewed Commonwealth in return for amnesty. Following Rax's death at the Battle of Kharon in 5 ASE, Amedda signed the Astral Concordance—a peace treaty recognizing the Dominion's capitulation and the end of the Dominion Schism. Amedda himself was permitted to return to Ceryon as the head of a provisional government. Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of Commonwealth overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead.

Unlike previous Astral governments, the Renewed Commonwealth broke with millennia of tradition by not establishing Ceryon as their headquarters, instead preferring to designate other member worlds with the status of Astral capital through an electoral system. During the Renewed Commonwealth Era, Ceryon had become the capital of the Renewed Commonwealth as it was designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure. As part of the Renewed Commonwealth Senate, Ceryon enjoyed full senatorial representation and, by the time of the Cold War, was aligned with the worlds that identified as Centrists. However, by this point, Ceryon had actually fallen under the control of crime syndicates. Now a symbol of the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current era, gangs fought a bloody war for control by the time after the Battle of Crait.
