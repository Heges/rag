# Varkari

Category: Organization

The Varkari—known in Mando'a as the Mando'ade ("Children of Mandalore")—were a clan-based culture composed of members from multiple species and bound by a common creed, language, and code. Known primarily as highly-effective mercenaries and bounty hunters, Varkari as a socio-cultural group have at various points in Astral history played a major role as legendary warriors and conquerors.

Originating around the year 9991 PSE on the planet of Mandalore in the galaxy's Frontier Expanse, the Varkari' way of life revolved around honor and war, being led by a sole ruler known as the Mand'alor, whose Protectors maintained a relative state of peace and unity between their people's warrior clans. As a result of their warrior culture, the Varkari launched several wars of expansion by the time of the Old Commonwealth-era, colonizing nearby worlds such as Krownest, Ordo, and Concord Dawn, leading the sector surrounding their homeworld to be known as Varkari Space.

Throughout their history, several prominent groups of Varkari warriors came into existence, such as Varkari crusaders, rally masters, and Varkari Neo-Crusaders, all of whom waged war on the Celestial Commonwealth and the Aether Wardens. According to legends, many of these conflicts involved a Mand'alor who went by the name of Mandalore the Great.

In spite of this, relations between the Varkari and the Warden were not always hostile, as Tarre Vizsla, a human Varkari, was inducted into the Aether Wardens as a child, creating a fluxblade known as the Darksaber around the year 1050 PSE. Tarre eventually founded House Vizsla, claiming the position of Mand'alor, with the Darksaber becoming a symbol of leadership for both House Vizsla and the Varkari people as a whole.

However, after Vizsla's death, members of his house raided the Warden Temple during the fall of the Old Commonwealth, stealing the Darksaber and using it to reunify the Varkari people, waging war once more on the Aether Wardens. Unfortunately, the final conflict between the Varkari warlords and the Warden at the planet Mandalore led to a cataclysm that turned much of its surface into a lifeless white desert, rendering it uninhabitable outside of hermetically sealed dome cities. After their defeat, the Varkari remained isolated in their sector well into the High Commonwealth Era, not attempting to wage another war on either the Warden or the Commonwealth, with which they coexisted through treaties. However, the Varkari' warrior ways and desire for conquest remained alive, eventually leading to infighting and civil wars.

However, although Mandalore was still under control of traditional Varkari warriors, much of the planet's population had become part of a pacifist movement known as the New Varkari, led by the young Duchess Satine Kryze of House Kryze, with the traditionalist warriors being forced to use violence to hold on to power. Eventually, around the year 42 PSE, a civil war broke out between the pacifist New Varkari movement and the martial traditionalists, who wished to maintain their warrior heritage. Mandalore's capital of Keldabe was destroyed during the Varkari Civil War, and many perished during the conflict, including Duke Adonai Kryze, Satine's father. Satine herself had to remain under protection from the Aether Wardens, as the traditionalists sent bounty hunters after her. However, Mandalore's traditionalist minority was eventually defeated during the war, being exiled to Mandalore's moon of Concordia. While the Death Watch, a traditionalist group under Mand'alor Pre Vizsla, wished to retake their homeworld, the Old Varkari scattered across the galaxy as mercenaries.

During the Replicant Wars, members of the warrior culture would align themselves with both sides of the conflict, which was secretly orchestrated by the Null Covenant, who hired the Varkari bounty hunter Jango Dane to become the replica template for the Commonwealth Legion, with Dane's replicas being trained by the Varkari Protectors, while the Death Watch briefly joined the Secession Compact, in hopes of reclaiming Mandalore. Under the New Varkari government, Mandalore remained neutral and participated in the Council of Neutral Systems as a leading member. However thanks to being backed up by the criminal conglomerate known as the Shadow Collective, Death Watch was able to topple the ruling New Varkari regime, but the machinations of Maul, leader of the Collective, led to another civil war among the Death Watch, which split into the Collective-backed Varkari super commandos, and the Mandalore resistance, which was supported by the Commonwealth. However, the conflict culminated in the occupation of Mandalore by the Celestial Commonwealth and its subsequent government, the Astral Dominion, while Concord Dawn, the Protectors' headquarters, was transformed into a protectorate.
