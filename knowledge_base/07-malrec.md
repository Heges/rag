# Malrec

Category: Character

Malrec, born Malrec and also simply known as the Emperor, was a male human Dark Lord of the Nullborn and Emperor of the Astral Dominion, with his reign lasting from 19 PSE to 4 ASE. Rising to power in the Astral Senate as the senator of Navara, the secretive Nullborn Lord cultivated two identities, Malrec and Malrec, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of the Celestial Commonwealth and the Aether Wardens through the Replicant Wars, and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the Battle of Elandra. However, the shadow current of Synth Flux gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Emperor was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Battle of Nexaris by Nira, his granddaughter born from his Strand-Cast replica son Dathan and his daughter-in-law Miramir.

Born on the Mid Rim planet Navara in 84 PSE as the High Commonwealth Era ended, Malrec possessed a strong connection to Synth Flux, and was secretly trained in the ways of the Nullborn by Null-Lord Plagueis with Malrec taking Malrec as his Nullborn persona. He eventually betrayed and killed his Nullborn Master, in order to train his own Nullborn apprentice, Null-Lord Varek, for the Rule of Two limited their ranks to two Nullborn Lords at any given time. The Invasion of Navara, a crisis orchestrated by Malrec, led to the Senate to elect Malrec as First Chancellor, although this came at the loss of Maul, who was defeated and presumed killed in the Battle of Navara. The Secessionist Crisis and the Replicant Wars were masterminded by Malrec and his second apprentice, Null-Lord Tyranus, in an effort to achieve Astral dominance. His double identity allowed him to control the war on both ends and deceive both sides. Following an attempt to arrest the chancellor, the Great Warden Purge commenced with Malrec enacting Directive Black.

With the Warden all but extinct, Malrec installed the New Order in the Commonwealth's place, declaring himself Astral Emperor and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy. Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Tyranus, he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Warden Knight Ardan Velgor to the dark side and transforming him into Xarn Velgor. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the Free Systems Compact. During the Dominion Schism, the Emperor sought to betray Velgor, by replacing him with the latter's son, Kael Orin. However, Malrec' plan backfired when Velgor ultimately renounced the dark side and killed Malrec to protect Kael. Thus, with the Nullborn's destruction, Synth Flux was brought back into balance, fulfilling Ardan's destiny as the prophesied Flux Heir.

Through his master's teachings, however, Malrec transferred his essence to an imperfect replica vessel that had been modeled on his original body. Anticipating the possibility of defeat, he created the Contingency to destroy the Astral Dominion and replace it with a new Nullborn Dominion, in the event of such a setback. His plans culminated in the destruction of the Renewed Commonwealth and the emergence of the New Dominion, the creation of Supreme Leader Sevrak, who turned Tal Voss—heir to the Orin bloodline—to the dark side, and the establishment of the Final Order. When the Emperor announced his return to the galaxy in 35 ASE, the Resistance invaded the planet Nexaris, Malrec' hidden stronghold in the Veiled Regions. During the battle, the resurrected Emperor discovered a dyad in Synth Flux in the form of the bond between Voss and Nira, a Warden initiate sired from the Malrec bloodline. His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when Nira called upon the strength of past Warden to vanquish her grandfather by turning his own power against him, destroying Malrec and his Nullborn Eternal cultists.
