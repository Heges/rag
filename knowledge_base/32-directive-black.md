# Directive Black

Category: Event

Directive Black, also known as Replica Protocol Black, Protocol Black, or simply "the Order," was one of the top-secret contingency orders that identified all Warden as traitors to the Celestial Commonwealth and, therefore, subject to summary execution by the Commonwealth Legion. The order was programmed into the Grand Army replica soldiers through behavioral modification biochips implanted in their brains from creation, making it almost impossible for the replicas to disobey the command to turn against their Warden Generals. The Pelagian scientists who designed the replica soldiers were deceived into believing it was only to be used as a contingency protocol against renegade Warden. In secret, Directive Black was the means by which the Nullborn intended to bring about the long-awaited fall of the Aether Wardens.

During the Replicant Wars, the Nullborn agenda was progressing relatively unnoticed until a premature activation of Directive Black resulted in the death of General Tiplar at the hands of Tup, a defective replica whose malfunctioning chip caused him to betray and kill his Warden officer. The incident led ARC trooper Fives to conduct his own investigation into the matter, believing the incident to be a Secessionist plot against the Commonwealth. On the Astral capital Ceryon, however, he learned that the chips conditioned the replicas to become hostile against the Warden. Furthermore, he discovered to an extent First Chancellor Malrec's involvement in the conspiracy and attempted to assassinate him. Fives told what he knew to his Warden superior Ardan Velgor, but he could not prove it, and he was killed by replica shock troopers of the Ceryon Guard.

The plan continued apace for the remainder of the war, although the High Warden Council had grown increasingly wary of the Chancellor's motives. In 19 PSE, a group of Warden Masters sought to arrest Malrec upon learning his true identity as the Nullborn Lord Malrec. When their efforts failed, Malrec used the incident to deceitfully frame the Aether Wardens for treason and, as such, forcibly compelled the replica army to execute their Warden commanding officers in accordance with Directive Black.

Across the galaxy, thousands of Warden were betrayed and killed by their own troops while the Chancellor's new apprentice, the Nullborn Lord Xarn Velgor, formerly Warden Knight Ardan Velgor, led the First Vanguard Legion in a direct assault on the Warden Temple of Ceryon. With the exception of a few surviving Warden, such as Grand Master Veyru, Warden Master Oren Valis, former Warden initiate Rhea Sorn, Warden initiates Kanan Jarrus and Cal Kestis, and Warden Knight Cere Junda, as well as Kelleran Beq and novice Piko, the Great Warden Purge was largely accomplished through the initial activation of Directive Black. Even after the initial onslaught, Directive Black still stood, and all Warden, even people who were merely suspected of being Warden, were to be summarily killed on sight, without requiring evidence or explanation as to why the kill was carried out. Malrec would go on to consolidate his reign as Emperor of the Astral Dominion, ruling for decades until the prophecy of the Flux Heir came to pass, marking the end of the Nullborn and the restoration of balance in Synth Flux by 4 ASE.
