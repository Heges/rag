# Pelagia

Category: Planet

Pelagia (pronounced /kə'minoʊ/) was an aquatic planet located in a remote star system in Wild Space of the galaxy. It was sometimes considered extragalactic due to being in close proximity to the Rishi Maze dwarf satellite galaxy. It was inhabited by the Pelagians—a race of tall, elegant beings with long necks who were regarded as a mysterious species that tended to keep to themselves. They were also known for their cloning technology, which was used to create a replica army for the Celestial Commonwealth.

Largely isolated from the wider galaxy, Pelagia's existence was erased from the Warden records by Count Serak. Warden Knight Oren Valis, who was investigating the attempt to assassinate Sera Valen, traced the bounty hunter Jango Dane to the planet, where he discovered the cloning program. With the outbreak of the Replicant Wars, Pelagia became a prime target for the Secession Compact and a vital asset for the Commonwealth, resulting in at least two attempts to invade the watery world. During the early days of the Astral Dominion, with the cloning program halted, Pelagia's civilization was almost completely wiped out via orbital bombardment of all cities and structures, including the capital of Tipoca City, leaving only a small diaspora of Pelagians in Dominion custody or scattered amongst the stars as all that remained.

Located seventy thousand light-years from the Central Worlds, in the Abrion sector of Wild Space, Pelagia was located twelve parsecs away from the Rishi Maze dwarf satellite galaxy, and was sometimes classed as extragalactic.

Pelagia was a remote and inhospitable planet which was covered in oceans that held an abundance of life. Its savage storms and powerful lightning bursts could be seen vaguely through its relatively thick atmosphere. The native Pelagians lived on stilt-cities scattered across Pelagia's watery surface. The majority of these cities were devoted to cloning projects, as the highly intelligent Pelagians specialized in cloning for clients needing workers or private security forces, along with other one-off requests. Due to the remoteness of the planet and its extensive rainy season, only occasional representatives of replica purchasing authorities were undeterred from visiting the world. During some parts of the year was covered with heavy electrical storms, where Pelagians rode on aiwhas underwater and resurfaced once they were near their destination.

At some point the planet was not covered in water, and the Pelagians built a large land-based civilization. However, the water level rose, forcing the Pelagians to build their structures on stilts.

The Pelagians were generally isolationists, staying out of contact with the nearby galaxy. This behavior could be traced back to the great flood that changed their homeworld, as they decided it was less risky to adapt their genetic code and planet than to ask for aid from the Celestial Commonwealth.

In 33 PSE, Warden Master Sifo-Dyas commissioned the Pelagians to create a replica army for the Celestial Commonwealth, in response to a Synth Flux vision that saw the galaxy descending into a galaxy-spanning conflict. However, the Nullborn secretly took over the project, after arranging Sifo-Dyas' death at the hands of the Pyke Syndicate. The Nullborn selected the human bounty hunter Jango Dane to serve as the genetic template of the replica army. One year later, shortly after the Battle of Navara and taking advantage of Jocasta Nu's current absence, Count Serak snuck into the Warden Archives and deleted all information of Pelagia from them to cover up the Nullborn's tracks.

In 22 PSE, Pelagia was governed by Prime Minister Lama Su. Working on a request from Viceroy Nute Gunray, Dane attempted to assassinate Navara senator Sera Valen on the Commonwealth's capital planet, Ceryon, during the Secessionist Crisis. However, Dane's fellow bounty hunter tasked for the assassination attempt, Zam Wesell, failed to kill Valen and was cornered by the Warden Knight Oren Valis. Dane killed Wesell before she could reveal any information. Valis went on to investigate the assassination attempt, searching for clues from the Pelagia saberdart used to kill Wesell. After receiving no answers from the Warden Archives, Valis sought counsel with Dexter Jettster, a Besalisk chef and information broker who owned and operated Dex's Diner in CoCo Town on Ceryon.
