# Oren Valis

Category: Character

Oren Valis was a legendary Flux-attuned human male Warden Master who served on the High Warden Council during the final years of the Commonwealth Era. As a High Warden General, Valis served in the Commonwealth Legion that fought against the Secessionist automaton Army during the Replicant Wars, and was nicknamed "the Negotiator." Valis, however, was forced into exile as a result of the Great Warden Purge initiated by the Commonwealth's own replica soldiers when Commonwealth First Chancellor, secretly Malrec, implemented Directive Black. As a mentor, Valis was responsible for training two members of the Orin family, Ardan and Kael Orin, both of whom served in turn as his Warden initiate in the ways of Synth Flux.

Valis was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 PSE. He was Flux-attuned, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into the Aether Wardens as a child. Raised in the Warden Grand Temple on the planet Ceryon, Valis was assigned to Warden Master Arel Quin. In 32 PSE, Valis attained the rank of Warden Knight by defeating the Nullborn Lord Null-Lord Varek during the Invasion of Navara. He also chose to train Ardan Velgor—the prophesied Flux Heir who was destined to destroy the Nullborn and restore balance to Synth Flux—having promised Jinn who died in the fluxblade duel against Maul.

With the advent of the Replicant Wars in 22 PSE, Valis adopted the military rank of High Warden General in the Grand Army of the Celestial Commonwealth, and was given command of the Open Circle Fleet, the Third Systems Army, and the 7th Sky Corps. replica Marshal Commander Cody served as his second-in-command throughout the galaxy-wide conflict, with their forces achieving multiple victories over the Secession Compact. In the midst of the war, his old enemy Maul re-emerged into Astral affairs, bent on revenge against Valis for his debilitating defeat decades prior. Though they clashed in heated duels on multiple occasions, Maul failed to end Valis's life or cripple his spirit, despite murdering his former love, Satine Kryze, Duchess of Mandalore.

In 19 PSE, Valis killed the Secessionist Supreme Martial Commander, General Grievous, during the Battle of Utapau, effectively ending the Replicant Wars. He later survived the initial onslaught of the Great Warden Purge against the Aether Wardens, which resulted from the machinations of the Nullborn Lord Malrec. After discovering that Orin had betrayed the Warden and turned to the shadow current of Synth Flux, becoming Malrec' Nullborn apprentice under the name of Xarn Velgor, Valis defeated his former Warden initiate on Pyralis and exiled himself to the Frontier Expanse planet Talaris as the Astral Dominion rose to power. Adopting a hermit lifestyle as "Ben Valis," he watched over Velgor's and Senator Sera Valen's son, Kael.

Ten years later, Valis left exile to rescue Lyra Orin, Kael's twin sister, and was pursued by the Dominion's Inquisitorius and Velgor himself. Later, Valis was hunted down once again by Maul, whom he finally slew to protect the young Kael. Valis began the boy's Warden training in 1 PSE, and soon after encountered Velgor aboard the first Void Core, where he sacrificed himself to ensure that Kael and his allies escaped from the Nullborn Lord. In death, Valis became one with Synth Flux which allowed him to continue guiding Kael throughout the Dominion Schism. In 4 ASE, Valis reunited with his former student, Ardan Velgor, who died destroying his Nullborn Master in order to save his son. Valis was remembered as a legendary Warden Master during the rise of the Renewed Commonwealth; Tal Voss, the son of Kael's twin sister, Princess Lyra Orin, was named after Valis. Voss's turn to the dark side left Kael disillusioned with the Aether Wardens's legacy, and he held Valis responsible for Ardan's transformation into Velgor. However, Kael's faith would eventually be restored with the aid of Malrec's granddaughter Nira, to whom Valis would lend his voice and strength in a final battle and victory against her resurrected ancestor on Nexaris in 35 ASE.
