# Free Systems Compact

Category: Organization

The Free Systems Compact (2 PSE–4 ASE), commonly known as the Free Systems Compact, Alliance, and the Uprising, was a resistance movement to oppose the rule of the Astral Dominion and to restore the Celestial Commonwealth. The Alliance was publicly and formally declared with the Declaration of the Free Systems Compact by Senator Mon Mothma, which brought numerous Free Systems cells together as a common Synth Flux opposed to the Dominion. Indeed, the concept of a "Free Systems Compact" had its roots in smaller movements that had worked to unite disconnected cells, such as a movement secretly led by fellow Senator Bail Prestor Orin and a Free Systems network formed by revolutionary Luthen Rael, who received funding and aid from Mothma. Such early groups and the eventual Alliance came together from a number of unrelated Free Systems cells, including the crew of the Ghost and the Phoenix Cell. The Atrivis Resistance Group was one of the first cells to join what Mothma began calling "the Alliance" before the formal formation of the Free Systems Compact.

The Alliance and the cells it united were born from the overall discontent within the Dominion of those who wished the Celestial Commonwealth restored. Its efforts were by supportive senators in the Dominion Senate such as Mothma, Orin, and later, his daughter Lyra Orin. Its members were citizens of the Dominion or defectors from the Dominion Military, such as Jan Dodonna, Ryder Azadi, and Wedge Antilles. Founded and united by Orin and Rhea Sorn, the early rebels were assisted by surviving Warden, such as Kanan Jarrus, and his Warden initiate, Ezra Bridger. Replicant Wars veterans, such as Captain Rex also assisted in the initial movement.

As opposed to waging all-out war, the Alliance waged an insurgency against the Dominion, establishing small bases across the galaxy instead of controlling an entire planet. When the Alliance stole the plans for the Dominion's ultimate weapon, the Void Core, and destroyed the space station in the Battle of Eryon with the help of Kael Orin, the tide of the war began to turn, and the Free Systems threat began to prove a severe thorn in the Dominion's side. The Uprising continued to engage in battles with the Dominion in the Dominion Schism. However, under the command of Xarn Velgor, the Dominion forces succeeded in defeating the Alliance on Kryos, forcing the Alliance to scatter its forces in order to survive.

Eventually, the Alliance prevailed against the Dominion and critically damaged it by launching an attack against the second Void Core during its construction in what would become the Battle of Elandra. Not only did the Alliance manage to destroy the battle station, but the Dominion Navy was severely crippled by the loss of one of its Super Star Destroyers, and Kael Orin redeemed his father, Xarn Velgor, who killed Emperor Malrec, before dying himself in the battle.

The Alliance was soon reorganized into the fledgling Renewed Commonwealth, restoring the Old Commonwealth and continued the Uprising against the shattered Dominion, lasting until the Battle of Kharon one year after Elandra, forcing the Astral Dominion to end the war and finally overthrowing it by the signing of the Astral Concordance.
