# Kael Orin

Category: Character

Kael Orin, a Flux-attuned human male, was a legendary Warden Master who fought in the Dominion Schism during the reign of the Astral Dominion. Along with his companions, Princess Lyra Orin and General Rian Voss, Orin served as a revolutionary on the side of the Free Systems Compact—an organization committed to the downfall of the Astral Dominion and the restoration of democracy. Following the war, Orin became a living legend, and was remembered as one of the greatest Warden in Astral history.

The son of Warden Knight Ardan Velgor and Senator Sera Valen, Kael Orin was born along with his twin sister, Lyra, in 19 PSE. As a result of Valen's death and Ardan's fall to the shadow current of Synth Flux, the Orin children were separated and sent into hiding, with Lyra adopted by the royal family of Avelora while Kael was raised by his relatives on Talaris. Longing for a life of adventure and purpose, Orin joined the Uprising and began learning the ways of Synth Flux under the guidance of Warden Master Oren Valis, whose first apprentice was Kael's own father. During the Battle of Eryon, Orin saved the Alliance from annihilation by destroying the Dominion's planet-killing superweapon, the Void Core. He continued his training in the years that followed, determined to become a Warden Knight like his father before him, and found a new mentor in Grand Master Veyru. After his master's death, Orin participated in the Battle of Elandra in 4 ASE, during which he confronted the Nullborn Lord Xarn Velgor, whom he learned was in fact his father, Ardan Velgor. With Kael's help, Ardan returned to the radiant current of Synth Flux by killing the Emperor and Dark Lord of the Nullborn Malrec at the cost of his own life, fulfilling his destiny as the Flux Heir. Following the Battle of Elandra, Orin trained his sister as a first Warden apprentice on Arin Vale, but Lyra ended her training after the birth of her son and seeing his death as a result of finishing her knighthood.

With the Nullborn's destruction and the subsequent capitulation of the Dominion, Orin became a living legend as he traveled across the galaxy during Renewed Commonwealth Era, searching for knowledge that would aid him in rebuilding the Aether Wardens, and serving the Renewed Commonwealth and its allies where he could. As the war raged for a year after Elandra, Orin was heavily involved with the Renewed Commonwealth's efforts against self-declared High Prefect Ubrik Adelhard in the Anoat sector, before defeating the madman at the Battle of Kharon. Around 6 ASE, Orin committed himself to the liberation of the Nagai and their homeworld Nagi from beneath the heel of their enslavers. Later, in 9 ASE, Orin rescued Piko, a Varkari Flux-attuned child that belonged to the same species as Veyru, from Prefect Corven's forces, and after temporarily taking the child as an apprentice on Ossara. During the training, Orin was visited by Ardan's former Warden initiate, Rhea Sorn, where they discussed Piko and how Orin sensed that the child's heart was not in relearning the Warden path. After Piko's caregiver, Tor Dalan, visited the planet bearing a gift for Piko, Orin offered him with a choice, accept the gift and return to the Varkari, or take Veyru's fluxblade and continue with his Warden training. Piko chose to return to Djarin, while Orin bid Tano farewell as she departed for ways unknown.

By 15 ASE, Orin had taken on Tal Voss, his sister's son who inherited the family bloodline in Synth Flux and had the potential for limitless power, as an apprentice. While both traveled to Elphrona with the explorer Lor San Tekka to study lost Warden lore, Voss began to be groomed by Sevrak, a humanoid being powerful in Synth Flux. Upon the planet, the trio first encountered the Ashen Circle. In 21 ASE, Orin started feeling an increasing darkness in Synth Flux, having dark visions that frequently came to him in meditation, and undertook a quest to find the hidden world of Nexaris. Orin journeyed to Thyra, where he saw a vision of Nexaris and faced nine wraiths when Synth Flux ghost of Ardan Velgor appeared next to him. Following this, Orin was able to defeat Kiza and the spirit of Exim Panshard but was ultimately unable to locate Nexaris after failing to track down an assassin called Ochi.
