# Automaton

Category: Technology

automatons, less commonly known as robots and automatons, were mechanical beings that possessed artificial intelligence. They were used in a variety of roles and environments, often those considered too menial or too dangerous for other species, but also in fields that required extensive specialization and knowledge.

The earliest origins of automatons were lost to history, and, by the time of the Renewed Commonwealth, it was unknown exactly when the first automaton was created, though some historians, only taking into account the more advanced worlds, placed the origin of the first automatons to approximately 30,000 years before the Battle of Eryon. Nevertheless, for millennia, automatons served in Astral society alongside organic beings. According to the B1-series battle automaton author R0-GR, the bond between organic and mechanical lifeforms were tenuous with the former fearing that automatons would rise up against their masters. Though several automaton revolts took place, Roger claimed that an "unspoken understanding" between human and automaton cultures kept everyone working on the same team throughout the ages.

automatons existed as far back as the earliest days of the Aether Wardens, which was founded over 25,000 years before the Replicant Wars. One such ancient automaton, Professor Huyang, served the Warden for several millennia. As a result of this ancient coexistence with living beings, the galaxy often had an uneven relationship between organics and mechanicals. Records of uprisings and clashes between automatons and their organic overlords became increasingly common as automatons slowly became more important in Astral society. During the High Commonwealth Era, the Nihil welcomed automatons into their ranks. They also designed blaredroids and scav automatons as weapons.

During the pan-Astral conflict known as the Replicant Wars, the Secession Compact used a variety of battle automatons in its fight against the Celestial Commonwealth. This mass use of automatons in combat cemented the idea of the "automaton as an existential threat to biological life" narrative in the minds of the Commonwealth. automatons were not utilized by the Chiss Ascendancy by the time of the Replicant Wars. Ultimately, the Commonwealth won the war and was replaced by an authoritarian Astral Dominion. Under the new regime, many people in the galaxy were afraid or otherwise distrustful of automatons, due to their bad memories of the conflict.

The Astral Dominion fostered anti-automaton sentiment and treated automatons as property. Many sentients continued the Replicant Wars policies of wiping automatons' memories, fitting them with restraining bolts, and curtailing their movements. Several establishments also excluded automatons. Further, anti-automaton sentiment adversely affected the labor fields dedicated to automaton design and manufacture, which forced those specialized in those fields—such as Maela's mother—to begin working in Dominion automaton factories in order to maintain a job. Otherwise, there were simply not enough openings in the market for those skills. After the Replicant Wars, the Dominion Senate also passed a law prohibiting programming that would allow for the harming of organics in the automatons used by the Dominion. Thus, with both public opinion and the law against it, battle automaton production fell sharply. However, this law was bypassed by Arakyd Industries in conjunction with the Dominion Military by marketing their KX-series automatons as "security automatons".

The illegal sport of automaton-fighting was practiced on Nal Hutta and in the Lodge, where Replicant Wars-survivor Ralakili ran the fighting pits due to his hate of automatons. The Free Systems Compact, which opposed the Dominion, treated automatons not as property but as sentient beings. Several automatons were encouraged by the Uprising and carried out acts of resistance such as bypassing repressive programming, deactivating restraining bolts, and demanding freedom. The Uprising's acceptance of automatons as equals was continued by the Renewed Commonwealth and Resistance. Support automatons were units that existed at some stage of Astral history that provided various assistant roles in battle.
