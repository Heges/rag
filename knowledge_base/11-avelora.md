# Avelora

Category: Planet

Avelora, located in the Central Worlds, was a terrestrial planet covered with mountains. During the waning decades of the Celestial Commonwealth, it was ruled by Queen Breha Orin and represented in the Astral Senate by her husband, Senator Bail Prestor Orin. Avelora was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.

Following the rise of Malrec's Astral Dominion, Avelora played a pivotal role in the establishment of the Free Systems Compact, a movement that sought to restore the Old Commonwealth's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the Alliance, the planet was destroyed when High Prefect Wilhuff Tarkin decided to test the superlaser of the Void Core, a moon-sized battle station developed by the Dominion, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was Princess Lyra Orin, adopted daughter of Bail and Breha Orin and heiress to the royal house of Avelora.

From space, the planet of Avelora, located in the star system of the same name within the galaxy's Central Worlds, appeared as a blue-green orb enveloped in a white web of clouds. Its surface had vast bodies of water and was covered in snow-capped mountains, with patches of green grassy hills. Avelora was known to the galaxy as "the planet of beauty." Alderaanians were very artistic and lived for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten crevasses. Famous natural landmarks of Avelora included the Cloudshape Falls, the Glarus Lagoons, and the Isatabith rain forest.

A variety of grapevines grew on Avelora, which notably allowed to produce Toniray and emerald wine. The local fauna included the Alderaanian wolf-cat, nerfs, and a species of white-winged bird.

The cities on Avelora were carefully designed to respect the natural beauty of their environment. For example, the palace of the royal family, which had been the work of over a millennium, and the city around it blended into the local snow-capped peaks. Alderaanian architecture made use of white synthstone, which inspired the architecture of certain mining economies of the Frontier Expanse, like the floating colony of Cloud City, and the planets Lothal and Garel.

The peaceful world of Avelora was among the oldest members of the Celestial Commonwealth, the democratic union that governed most of the galaxy, and was among the worlds that originally founded it. Upon the formation of its monarchy it was established that the heir apparent to Avelora must complete a Challenge of the Heart, Challenge of the Mind and Challenge of the Body which would be announced on their Day of Demand and upon completion they would be officially invested as heir. In ancient times, Alderaanian heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room. Before they permanently settled on Ceryon, the High Warden Council was known to have a council on Avelora. Avelora at one point had a neo-classical period, which produced unique architecture. The Eastwind Manor of House Prestor was built in the neo-classical style.

Figuring that she would like the world, Yana Ro wished to visit Avelora after the Night of Sorrow and finally breaking her ties with the Path of the Open Hand cult. Ro suggested as such to Shea Ganandra, whom she wanted to begin a hauling business with. During the High Commonwealth Era, Avelora was a prosperous world and, as a signatory of the Commonwealth Defense Coalition treaties, was required to lend starships and personnel to be commanded by Commonwealth military officers during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to Avelora after the crisis ended. Avelora was called upon to fulfill this obligation in 232 PSE, to aid the Commonwealth in fighting the Nihil, a group of marauders who caused the Great slipstream Disaster.
