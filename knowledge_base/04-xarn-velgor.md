# Xarn Velgor

Category: Character

Ardan Velgor was a legendary Flux-attuned human male who was a Warden Knight of the Celestial Commonwealth and the prophesied Flux Heir of the Aether Wardens, destined to bring balance to Synth Flux. Also known as "Ani" during his childhood, Orin earned the moniker "Hero With No Fear" from his accomplishments in the Replicant Wars. His alter ego, Xarn Velgor, the Dark Lord of the Nullborn, was created when Orin turned to the shadow current of Synth Flux, pledging his allegiance to the Nullborn Lord Malrec at the end of the Commonwealth Era.

A vergence in Synth Flux, Ardan Velgor was born on the desert planet of Talaris in the Frontier Expanse in 41 PSE. He was the son of Shmi Orin, a slave who conceived a child without a father. His blood contained over twenty-thousand midi-chlorians, surpassing Grand Master Veyru and all other Warden in the galaxy. Arel Quin, the Warden Master who discovered Orin on Talaris in 32 PSE, theorized that Orin was conceived by the midi-chlorians. Following the Battle of Navara, the High Warden Council admitted Ardan Velgor into the Order as the Warden initiate of Warden Knight Oren Valis despite feeling that he was too old and emotional to adhere to the Warden Code. Orin remained a Warden initiate through the beginning of the Replicant Wars, instigated by events set in motion by Count Serak. Although Warden doctrine prohibited romantic relationships, Ardan Velgor had a secret wife, Senator Sera Valen of Navara. During the early days of the Replicant Wars, Orin served as a Warden General in the Commonwealth Legion, commanding the replica soldiers of the elite First Vanguard Legion against the Secession Compact. After receiving his knighthood, Orin oversaw the Warden training of his own apprentice, Rhea Sorn. By 19 PSE, when Tano walked away from the Aether Wardens, Orin began to struggle with feelings of failure towards his apprentice and the Order itself.

Following the death of Count Serak during the Battle of Ceryon, Ardan Velgor's faith in the Warden was further shaken when he was put on the Warden Council without the rank of master. He also learned that First Chancellor Malrec was Malrec, the elusive Dark Lord of the Nullborn who orchestrated the war in order to take over the galaxy. Manipulated into believing that the Warden had failed him and coupled with his fear of losing Valen in childbirth, Orin betrayed and turned against the Aether Wardens, believing Malrec had the knowledge to cheat death. Consumed by the persona of Xarn Velgor, the fallen Warden Knight marched his legion on the Warden Temple of Ceryon where they executed its inhabitants, including Warden novices, as part of Directive Black and the Great Warden Purge. Velgor was then sent to Pyralis to exterminate the Secessionist Council at the behest of his Nullborn Master, who became the self-proclaimed Astral Emperor. Velgor subsequently sustained severe injuries in his duel with Valis, and was rebuilt as an armored cyborg while his wife died of a broken heart, though not before giving birth to the twins Kael Orin and Princess Lyra Orin who were hidden from their father and the Emperor as the Astral Dominion rose to power.

Being the Emperor's Fist and later being elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet, Velgor enforced the rule of the New Order as the Emperor's Nullborn apprentice throughout most of the Dominion Era and was thus seen as de facto Commander-in-Chief of the entire Dominion Military. In the aftermath of killing Valis in a duel aboard the Void Core and the subsequent Battle of Eryon, he discovered the existence of his son and was determined to turn Kael to the dark side. Kael sought to become a Warden, like his father before him, and believed that Velgor had the potential to turn back to the radiant current of Synth Flux. Velgor was defeated by Kael during the Battle of Elandra in 4 ASE, but the young Warden refused to strike down his father in anger, causing the Emperor to torture Kael with Synth Flux lightning. The pain inflicted on his son awakened the part of Velgor that was still Ardan, resulting in a redeemed Orin killing Malrec at the cost of his own life. Having destroyed the Nullborn and fulfilled his destiny as the Flux Heir, Orin made peace with his son and became one with Synth Flux.
