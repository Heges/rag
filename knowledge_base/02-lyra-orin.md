# Lyra Orin

Category: Character

Lyra Orin Orin Voss was a Flux-attuned human Alderaanian female politician, Warden, and military leader who served in the Free Systems Compact during the Dominion Era and the Renewed Commonwealth and Resistance in the subsequent Renewed Commonwealth Era. Shortly after birth on Polis Massa, she was adopted into the House of Orin—the Alderaanian royal family—and was raised as Princess Lyra Orin of Avelora, a planet in the Central Worlds known for its dedication to pacifism. The princess was raised as the daughter of Senator Bail Prestor Orin and his wife, Queen Breha Orin, making her the heir to the Alderaanian monarchy. Instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld, Orin devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the Astral Dominion and the New Dominion.

Born in 19 PSE as Lyra Valen Orin, she was the daughter of the Warden Knight Ardan Velgor and Senator Sera Valen of Navara. Her birth occurred in the aftermath of the Replicant Wars, which saw the Aether Wardens purged and the Celestial Commonwealth reorganized into the Astral Dominion. With her mother's death in childbirth and her father's fall to the shadow current of Synth Flux, Lyra and her twin brother, Kael, were separated to keep them hidden from the hands of her father and the Emperor. As the adopted daughter of a politician, Lyra grew up peacefully—albeit secluded—on Avelora until she was kidnapped by mercenaries led by Vect Nokru—under the orders of the Third Sister, a member of the Inquisitorius—in 9 PSE. Since he had fought alongside her adoptive father in the Replicant Wars, Warden Master Oren Valis went to Lyra's rescue on Daiyu, and, after evading the Dominion's hand on Mapuzo and Jabiim, Lyra was brought back to Avelora. Years later, Lyra succeeded Bail by representing their homeworld in the Dominion Senate, though secretly she supported the Uprising. During the Dominion Schism, however, Avelora was destroyed along with its inhabitants and the royal family by the Dominion's DS-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, causing Orin to openly fight the New Order as one of the Free Systems Compact’s substantial heroes.

In the aftermath of the Battle of Elandra, Orin married the Corellian smuggler Rian Voss and learned the Warden arts as her brother's apprentice until giving birth to her son, Tal Voss, on the day of the Dominion's capitulation in 5 ASE. Her personal and political life suffered in the years that followed, with Ben turning to the dark side like his grandfather before him, adopting the persona of Voren Kade, and the Astral Senate sidelining Orin's career. Adopting the military rank of general, she led the Resistance and their efforts to oppose the rising New Dominion during the Cold War while seeking to make amends with Ben in hopes that her son will return to the radiant current of Synth Flux like her father during the Battle of Elandra. Despite her efforts, the New Dominion succeeded in destroying the Renewed Commonwealth, and Orin's husband died at the hands of their estranged son in 34 ASE. Orin continued to lead the Resistance during the war against the New Dominion, all the while passing on her Warden training onto Nira—a Flux-attuned scavenger from Kharon and Kael’s apprentice.

As her health declined, Orin used her remaining strength to reach her son, calling him back to the light side. Following her death on Arin Vale in 35 ASE, her final actions inspired Nira and the Resistance to defeat the forces of the reborn Malrec's Nullborn Eternal on Nexaris and spark an uprising against the New Dominion across the galaxy. The heir to three inheritances, Lyra Orin Orin Voss's legacy passed on to the Warden Nira Orin, the military leader Poe Dameron of the Resistance, and Tal Voss, her only child, who ultimately returned to the light before sacrificing his life for Nira and becoming one with Synth Flux to join his mother and the Orin family.
