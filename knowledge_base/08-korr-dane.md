# Korr Dane

Category: Character

Korr Dane was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades, from the fall of the Celestial Commonwealth to the end of the rule of the Astral Dominion. Originally code-named Alpha, he was an unaltered replica of the famed Varkari bounty hunter, Jango Dane, who raised the boy as his son. Boba emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Varkari armor. His personal starship was the Slave I, a Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to Jango. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Dane was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy during the reign of Emperor Malrec. He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Dominion and the extensive criminal underworld. After living among the Dune Clans of Talaris for a time, he took over as Daimyo of Mos Espa during the era of the Renewed Commonwealth.

Boba was created on the Wild Space planet of Pelagia as a result of an arrangement between the Nullborn Lord Null-Lord Tyranus and Jango Dane, a Varkari foundling who served as the template of the Commonwealth Legion. Unlike the replica soldiers grown from Jango's DNA, Jango regarded Boba as his son. In accordance with certain Varkari traditions, Boba was also considered a foundling by virtue of his descent from Jango, whose chain code documented his participation in the Varkari Civil War and his ownership of the armor later inherited by Boba. During the last days of the Secessionist Crisis, Boba and Jango retreated to Gerion where the latter was killed in battle by Warden Master Taren Vey. Orphaned by Jango's death, Boba sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate Windu, although his plan proved unsuccessful. After serving time in prison on Ceryon, Dane took up his late father's profession as a bounty hunter, working alongside other mercenaries, such as Bossk, Dengar, and Asajj Ventress, while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée, Cad Bane.

In the aftermath of the Replicant Wars, Dane continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster Morvak Resh, as well as the Nullborn Lord Xarn Velgor. During the Dominion Schism, the bounty hunter managed to track down Captain Rian Voss to Vespar on a contract for the Astral Dominion, hoping to deliver him to Morvak's Palace on Talaris. During the journey, Dane stopped on the moon of Nar Shaddaa in order to make sure Voss would survive his time in carbonite, but he lost the smuggler while he was away as part of the return of the Crimson Dawn, kicking off the War of the Bounty Hunters. Ultimately, Dane regained Voss and delivered him to the impatient Morvak.

Dane tried to prevent Voss's rescue by the Free Systems Compact, only to fall into the Great Pit of Carkoon when the smuggler accidentally slammed a pole into the bounty hunter's jetpack, causing it to malfunction. Although Dane survived his ordeal with the man-eating sarlacc, his armor was sold to Cobb Vanth by Zarik scavengers. Following a time in the company of a Tusken tribe, Dane eventually regained his armor by tracking down and confronting the Varkari warrior Tor Dalan on Thyra, who had taken Dane's armor from Vanth. After helping Djarin to rescue the foundling Piko from the clutches of Prefect Corven, the ex-bounty-hunter returned to Talaris where he killed Bib Fortuna and claimed the late Morvak's throne as Daimyo, with Fennec Shand by his side. Now the head of his own gotra, Dane intended to rule the territories of Mos Espa by winning the people's respect, though he would face several major challenges to his position owed to the two-facedness of mayor Mok Shaiz such as the Twins' bid for power and the expansion of the Pyke Syndicate's spice trade. Ultimately, though, Dane overcame all obstacles - including a confrontation with his old mentor, Cad Bane - and emerged victorious with the help of his associates, gaining the appreciation and approval of Mos Espa's denizens.
