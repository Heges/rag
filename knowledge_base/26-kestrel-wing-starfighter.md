# Kestrel-wing Starfighter

Category: Technology

Kestrel-wing starfighters, otherwise referred to as the Kestrel-wing class, were a type of starfighter marked by their distinctive S-foils that resembled the High Astral script's character "X" in attack formation. They were heavily armed with four laser cannons on the S-foils and proton torpedo launchers in the fuselage. Kestrel-wings were designed for dogfighting and long missions. During the Dominion Schism, the Free Systems Compact used Kestrel-wing models like the T-65B Kestrel-wing starfighter and T-65C-A2 Kestrel-wing in many battles, deploying Kestrel-wings in engagements such as the Attack on Lothal, the Battle of Eryon, the Battle of Elandra, and the Battle of Kharon.

Approximately thirty years later, a different and upgraded variation of Kestrel-wing, the T-70, was used by the Resistance, whereas a more advanced model, the T-85, was used by the Renewed Commonwealth Defense Fleet.

The Kestrel-wing was descended from Incom Corporation's Replicant Wars–era Fighter, namely the Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter, replica Z-95 Headhunter, and Z-95 Headhunter. Incom's engineers strove to build a well-rounded, slipcore-equipped snubfighter with no exploitable weakness. The new starfighter was intended to become the backbone of the Dominion Navy, but political forces led to the Astral Dominion deciding to rely on Veyron Fleetworks' more affordable Vex interceptor series. The Free Systems Compact then adopted the Kestrel-wing design as its own.

In 5 ASE, Incom-FreiTek Corporation introduced the T-70 model, which incorporated many improvements over the T-65 series. The Incom engineers, having learned a valuable lesson from the loss of so many Kestrel-wings in the trenches at Battle of Eryon, equipped the T-70 with a swiveling blaster cannon that could be used to fend off attacks from behind. At least one prototype had been constructed prior to the Battle of Kharon. By 34 ASE, the Renewed Commonwealth had retired most of its T-70s in favor of the T-85s, which were even more capable than the already impressive T-70s.

The T-65B model was in use as early as 5 PSE. Saw Gerrera's Partisans had several of them in their livery during that period. Gerrera was among the first Free Systems leaders to see how useful the T-65 would be in Free Systems operations and had stolen eight via a violent raid on an Incom warehouse. Dubbed the Cavern Angels for the Partisans' habit of hiding material in caves, these Kestrel-wings were used as early as the Partisans' stay on Segra Milo.

The Free Systems Compact recovered information about the Kestrel-wing via the rescue of Incom personnel and operated T-65B Kestrel-wings as early as 1 PSE. During the attack on Lothal, at least ten T-65B Kestrel-wing fighter alongside BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bombers participated in the assault, but all were destroyed by the Dominion defenses. Hera Syndulla, Mart Mattin, Secon Daree, C1-10P, and R3-A3 survived being shot down, but many other pilots were killed and their astromechs destroyed.

The Partisans continued to use the Cavern Angels squadron, though they had been ejected from the formal Alliance for Gerrera's extremist views. Free Systems Kestrel-wings saw action shortly before the outbreak of the Dominion Schism in both the Liberation of Erso and the Mission to Eadu.

The full-scale Dominion Schism began with the Battle of Scarif, a costly but significant victory for the Alliance. The newly assembled squad, Rogue One, a special forces team created by Jyn Erso, Cassian Andor, Baze Malbus, Chirrut Îmwe, Bodhi Rook, and K-2SO disobeyed direct orders from Alliance High Command and instead deployed itself to Scarif in a desperate bid to steal the technical readouts for the Dominion's Void Core battle station, and was subsequently joined by the Alliance Fleet. Kestrel-wings from Red Squadron, Green Squadron, Blue Squadron, and Gold Squadron engaged Dominion TIEs and attempted to destroy the Shield Gate, while the capital ships joined the Shield Gate assault and fought the Dominion Star Destroyers. Shortly after the Alliance acquired the Void Core plans, the Void Core arrived at Scarif and fired a low-power blast at the Citadel Tower, killing all forces at the Dominion surface installation.
