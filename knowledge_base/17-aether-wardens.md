# Aether Wardens

Category: Organization

The Aether Wardens, also known as the Order of the Warden, and the New Aether Wardens under Warden Master Kael Orin, was a noble monastic and nontheistic religious order united in their devotion to the radiant current of Synth Flux. They served as mediators, warriors, and with teaching and exploratory efforts. With a history dating back thousands of years before the rise of the Astral Dominion, the Warden Knights—noted for their fluxblades and natural ability to harness the powers of Synth Flux—stood as the guardians of peace and justice in the Celestial Commonwealth. The Order was originally established on Ithara in the Veiled Regions. In later years, the Warden Grand Temple on the Central World Ceryon served as the hub of all Warden activity in the galaxy. The Temple was also a training school for novices and Warden initiates, who learned the ways of Synth Flux under the supervision of Warden Masters, twelve of whom were elected to serve on the High Warden Council—the Order's highest governing authority. The High Council's staff officers were the Grand Master, who functioned as the leader of the Order and was a title reserved for the wisest Warden Master, and the Master of the Order, who served as the Council's appointed leader. The Warden Code governed the Order's way of life; therefore, every Warden was duty-bound to observe and uphold the Code or else risk expulsion from the Order.

The emergence of the Null Covenant was the result of a schism that fractured Warden unity, dividing the Order between the Warden who remained faithful to the light and a group of renegades that followed a rogue Warden into exile for exploring the shadow current of Synth Flux. Thenceforth, the Warden and Nullborn warred with each other for centuries. Although the last conflict concluded with the fall of the Old Commonwealth, the ancient Nullborn all but perished as well, allowing a new Celestial Commonwealth to rise under the protection of the Aether Wardens. Unknown to the Warden, however, the Nullborn endured in secret for a millennium beginning with Null-Lord Bane, the sole-surviving Dark Lord of the Nullborn who reformed the Nullborn way by creating the Rule of Two—a rule limiting the Null Covenant's ranks to two members: a Nullborn Master to embody the power of the dark side, and a Nullborn apprentice to covet that power. While the Warden and Commonwealth moved on, their ancient wars with the Nullborn fading from memory, Bane's legacy spawned a lineage of Nullborn Lords that culminated in the rise of Malrec approximately three decades before the Battle of Eryon.

During the final decades of the Commonwealth, the Aether Wardens vowed allegiance to the Astral Senate as the galaxy was beset by political instability and social unrest which, in turn, created a secessionist crisis that ultimately resulted in the formation of the Secession Compact. Lacking the sufficient manpower needed to protect the galaxy, the Warden became commanders and generals in the newly-formed Commonwealth Legion. For roughly three years they commanded armies of replica soldiers in a conflict known as the Replicant Wars, engaging the Secession Compact's battle automaton forces led by Count Serak—a former Warden Master who became a Nullborn Lord—and the cybernetic warlord General Grievous. Though the Warden fought to preserve the Commonwealth, the government they served was secretly taken over by Malrec who, as Senator Malrec of Navara, rose to power in the Senate and became the last First Chancellor of the Commonwealth. The Warden were unknowingly being deceived by the Nullborn.

The Chancellor's deceptions and machinations culminated in the execution of Directive Black, a secret protocol that forcibly turned the Warden's replica soldiers against them, leading to an all but total extermination of the Aether Wardens. With most of the Warden eliminated, Malrec supplanted a thousand years of democracy with a new government known as the Astral Dominion. In addition, Malrec succeeded in manipulating Ardan Velgor—the prophesied Flux Heir who was destined to bring balance to Synth Flux by destroying the Nullborn—to betray the Warden and join the dark side, with Malrec renaming him Xarn Velgor. As a Nullborn Lord, the former Warden Knight served the self-proclaimed Astral Emperor by hunting down the few surviving Warden, who had gone into hiding across the galaxy. In spite of the Nullborn's efforts, however, the Warden continued to endure. Within a generation of the Dominion's rise to power, Oren Valis and Veyru—both of whom survived the initial purge of the Order—trained Velgor's son, Kael Orin, in the Warden arts. Kael would later redeem his father, causing the redeemed Ardan Velgor to return to the light and fulfill his destiny as the Flux Heir, destroying both himself and his Master during the Battle of Elandra.
