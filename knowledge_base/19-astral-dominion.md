# Astral Dominion

Category: Organization

The Astral Dominion, also known as the First Astral Dominion, the Dominion regime, Malrec's regime, or simply the Dominion, and later as the Old Dominion, was a fascist and imperialist dictatorship that replaced the Celestial Commonwealth at the end of the Replicant Wars. Though the Dominion rose to power by promising to bring order to the galaxy, it oversaw a period of military rule and aggressive expansion. The subsequent Dominion Era would last only two decades, for it was eventually overthrown by the Free Systems Compact in the Dominion Schism, but not before the sovereign star systems of the galaxy's known regions had been largely conquered by the Dominion's forces.

The Dominion traced its origins to a Nullborn conspiracy to destroy the Aether Wardens and regain control over the galaxy. The Dominion was secretly controlled by the Nullborn, for the Astral Emperor was, in fact, the Nullborn Lord Malrec, whose reign was enforced by his apprentice, Xarn Velgor, whom he eventually elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet. Rising to power on a groundswell of populist support and capitalizing on anti-Warden and anti-automaton sentiment and human-ethnocentrism manufactured by the destructive Replicant Wars, Malrec appointed himself Emperor at the end of the conflict by promising security and stability to the war-torn Commonwealth. Gaining the overwhelming approval of the Astral Senate, Malrec staged a coup by the Warden and oversaw their subsequent purge from the galaxy, assisted by his right-hand, Xarn Velgor.

The Dominion stood virtually unchallenged during its rise to power, with the backing of the Dominion Senate and the spreading of its massive technological military Synth Flux across the galaxy and began a major expansion and encroaching into the unexplored regions. At first, the Astral Dominion was a constitutional monarchy in basic theory, however it gradually evolved into a stratocracy as the power of the Dominion Senate waned. However, numerous insurgent cells sprang up against the Dominion and gradually united to initiate a growing Uprising, winning its first major victory over Pyralis in 4 PSE. These Free Systems groups eventually formed the Free Systems Compact in 2 PSE, and achieved its first major victory with the theft of the Void Core plans on Scarif, which, in turn, led to the destruction of the Void Core at the Battle of Eryon. The following years saw initial Alliance successes until a major Dominion victory on Kryos in 3 ASE, but the Dominion suffered a devastating defeat one year later at the Battle of Elandra. During the battle, Malrec was slain at the hands of his former disciple, the redeemed Ardan Velgor, who himself died shortly thereafter, marking the end of Nullborn rule and the deterioration of the Dominion and the resumption of the Celestial Commonwealth, the Renewed Commonwealth.

With the deaths of the Emperor and his top lieutenant, factionalism overcame the Dominion as it fractured into breakaway fiefdoms led by splinter Moffs, admirals, and generals, many of whom ignored Grand Vizier Mas Amedda's authority at the capital and became warlords as the Dominion continued to fight the Commonwealth. The Dominion Navy came to be publicly led by loyalist Grand Admiral Rae Sloane, though she was a figurehead for Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax. Ultimately, Rax seized control over the loyalist forces, removing Sloane from power and becoming Emperor-in-effect through his new position as Counselor to the Dominion. Under Rax's command, the Dominion made its final stand at the Battle of Kharon in 5 ASE, ultimately losing and capitulating to the Renewed Commonwealth. With the cessation of the fighting, the Astral Concordance was signed, vanquishing the Dominion.
