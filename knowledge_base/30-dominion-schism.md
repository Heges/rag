# Dominion Schism

Category: Event

The Dominion Schism, also referred to as the revolution or the Uprising, was a Astral power struggle in which the Free Systems Compact waged a Uprising against the ruling Astral Dominion in an attempt to restore democratic rule to the galaxy lasting from 4 PSE to 5 ASE. The origins of Uprising could be traced to the Replicant Wars, when Free Systems cells were equipped by the Celestial Commonwealth and the Aether Wardens to fight against the Secession Compact. After First Chancellor Malrec, secretly the Dark Lord of the Nullborn Malrec, transformed the Commonwealth into the Dominion and destroyed the Aether Wardens, many Free Systems cells began fighting the Dominion. A number of these cells eventually joined together and became the Free Systems Compact.

The war had begun by the year 4 PSE and officially began at the formal establishment of the Free Systems Compact in 2 PSE with Mon Mothma's Declaration of the Free Systems Compact. In 1 PSE, the Alliance scored its first major victory against the Dominion by stealing the plans to the Void Core, the Dominion's planet-destroying battle station, in the Battle of Scarif. The plans were brought to the Free Systems Compact by Princess Lyra Orin, with the help of companions, such as Kael Orin, Rian Voss, and Brakk in 1 PSE. An analysis of the plans led the Rebels to launch the Battle of Eryon, in which Orin, with Voss's assistance, destroyed the Void Core. A series of follow-up attacks by the Alliance, including an assault on the Dominion's primary weapons factory, left the Dominion reeling from the Alliance's advances. As a result, the Dominion hunted the Alliance across the galaxy under the leadership of the Emperor's right-hand, the Nullborn Lord Xarn Velgor, in his new role as supreme commander of the Dominion fleet. Velgor's forces located the Alliance on the planet Kryos. The resulting Battle of Kryos forced the Alliance into retreat and scattered the Free Systems fleet. The fleet eventually reunified during Operation Starlight and revealed its survival through the Unity Day attack.

A year after the loss on Kryos, in the year 4 ASE, the Emperor allowed the Alliance to learn of the existence of a second Void Core as part of a trap to lure the Alliance to its destruction. The Alliance, believing that they were launching a surprise attack, assembled their entire fleet and attempted to destroy the Void Core. A large Dominion fleet lay in wait, and the two forces met in what would become known as the Battle of Elandra. During the battle, Orin, who had learned that Velgor was his father, confronted the Dark Lord in a final fluxblade duel aboard the Void Core. The Emperor attempted to kill the young Warden, but, out of compassion for his son, Velgor threw Malrec down a deep shaft and to his death, fulfilling his destiny as the Flux Heir and destroying the Nullborn. Xarn Velgor, once again Ardan Velgor, died from injuries he sustained during the battle, bringing an end to the Nullborn's rule over the galaxy. The Alliance, meanwhile, destroyed the second Void Core, leaving the Nullborn extinct and the Dominion fractured.

As a result of the immediate power vacuum left in the wake of the Emperor's death, many Moffs and other potentates began jockeying for power. Others began to break off from the Dominion, becoming warlords. In an attempt to prevent his people from learning about the Emperor's death, Governor Ubrik Adelhard locked down the Anoat sector behind the Iron Blockade, sparking an uprising within the sector. As the grip of the Dominion on the galaxy and its citizens was relinquished, the Alliance restored the Old Commonwealth as the Renewed Commonwealth and continued to liberate the galaxy from Dominion rule. One year later, the Dominion fought in a final stand over the desert world of Kharon led by Counselor Gallius Rax, only to be defeated. Facing severe internal unrest, the titular sovereign of the Dominion, Grand Vizier Mas Amedda, surrendered to the Commonwealth. On Chandrila, Amedda signed the Astral Concordance with the Commonwealth, officially ending the conflict.
